This application includes the AndroidX Media3 FFmpeg audio decoder module.

Media3 version: 1.11.0
Source: https://github.com/androidx/media/tree/91fd5df7ab6b5c2f1a589fbdee5ee8deeae97f02/libraries/decoder_ffmpeg

The bundled FFmpeg libraries are built from FFmpeg 6.0 (commit
b4a62c32549b8295691a8e0ff2c9b82188923159) with the E-AC-3 decoder enabled.
