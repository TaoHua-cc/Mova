# Player surface brief

- Scope: Windows mpv desktop playback surface; Operate mode.
- Job: keep video dominant while making transport, resource, audio, danmaku, subtitles, speed, chapters, and released episodes immediately understandable and usable.
- Approved direction: Playback Console, based on `.impeccable/mocks/player-c-playback-console.png`.
- Composition: quiet title row at top; one lower structural-glass console with scrubber as its top edge; metadata left, transport centered, utilities right; contextual sheets open upward from their trigger.
- Signature state: episode list becomes one wide, shallow horizontal tray with five neighboring episodes and a bright current selection.
- Component grammar: 18px large surfaces, 10–12px compact controls, one soft inner highlight, no nested glass, coherent 1.75–2px outline icons, bright translucent-white selected state.
- Constraints: default windowed playback; every shown control must retain its mpv function and remain legible over changing video content.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/player-c-playback-console.png`.
